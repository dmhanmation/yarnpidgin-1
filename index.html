A quick introduction before your first lesson

Wetin be Pidgin English? Nigerian Pidgin English na language wey over 100 million Nigerians de use every day. E start for di 1700s when Portuguese traders come meet Nigerians for business. Today, Pidgin na di language of Afrobeats, comedy, football banter and everyday Naija life. If you sabi small Pidgin, you go connect wit Naija people on a whole different level!
